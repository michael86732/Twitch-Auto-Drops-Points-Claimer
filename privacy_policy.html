Privacy Policy for Twitch Auto Drops & Points Claimer

Effective Date: 2025-05-01

This Privacy Policy explains how the Twitch Auto Drops & Points Claimer Chrome extension ("the Extension") handles your information.

We Do Not Collect Any User Data

The Extension is designed to enhance your Twitch viewing experience by automating the claiming of Twitch Drops and Channel Points.

The Extension does NOT collect, store, transmit, or process any personal information or any other data from your browsing activity, your Twitch account, or your device.

The Extension operates solely within your browser on Twitch.tv pages to identify and interact with specific user interface elements (like claim buttons) for the purpose of automating reward collection. It does not connect to any external servers or services to send or receive your data.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
